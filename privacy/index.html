Privacy Policy

TripSloth is built to respect you. Here is exactly how the app handles information — in plain language.

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. The short version

No account. No ads. No tracking. We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information with any third party for advertising or marketing. There is no analytics SDK tracking your behavior, and no ad SDK at all. Your trip plans are stored on your device and — if you use iCloud — in your private iCloud storage, which only you can access.

The app does send trip-related text (your destination, dates, and interests) to a few services in order to do its job. Those flows are described below, and none of them includes your name, contacts, or any other personal identifier — because we never ask for one.

2. Trip requests (AI itinerary generation)

When you create a trip, the app sends your request — destination, number of days, interests, and the text you typed — to a third-party AI service that generates search queries and itinerary suggestions. That service processes requests under its own privacy terms. We send only the trip request itself, with no account information, name, email, or device identifiers attached.

3. Place discovery

To find real places, the app queries public data services with search terms and coordinates only:

  • Apple Maps — place and route searches.
  • Wikipedia and Wikivoyage — editorial content about attractions.
  • OpenStreetMap — geographic data and points of interest.

These requests contain only the location or place name being looked up. They are not linked to you.

4. Storage of your trips

Your trips and itineraries are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled on your device, they are also stored in your private iCloud database (via CloudKit) so they sync across your own devices. We operate no server that stores your trips, and we cannot see or access them.

5. Feature configuration

The app periodically fetches lightweight feature settings from a third-party configuration service. These are anonymous, device-level fetches only; they do not collect personal information or track you across apps or websites.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children, and since we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

7. Your control

You remain in control of your data:

  • Delete trips at any time from within the app.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app from your device (and the app's iCloud data, if you wish, via your iPhone's iCloud settings).
  • Disable iCloud sync by turning off iCloud for the app in your iPhone settings — the app then keeps trips on your device only.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be called out in the app's release notes.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us at apps@maadotaa.com and we'll be happy to help.
