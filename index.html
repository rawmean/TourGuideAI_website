Why TripSloth

Real places. Smart routes. Zero fuss.

Most planners hand you a generic list. TripSloth hand-builds your trip from places that actually exist — and deserve the visit.

Plan your way

Start from where you are, an address, or a dropped pin. Pick your days and your loves — food, history, art, nature — and watch a personal plan take shape.

Real places, quality-checked

Every stop is discovered from MapKit, Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, and OpenStreetMap — and only attractions with editorial or independent notability evidence make it in. No AI hallucinations.

Day-by-day itineraries

Each day laid out as a flowing route — what to see, in what order, with travel details between stops. Tweak anything and the plan adapts.

Maps that guide you

A trip-wide map with optimized routes and driving access points resolved for every stop — plus turn-by-turn navigation. Tilt the camera, switch map styles; it's yours to explore.

Trips that stick around

Every plan is saved to your device and your private iCloud — revisit last year's road trip or share a plan, any time.

Private by design

No account to create, no ads, no third-party tracking. Your trips are yours — stored on your device and in your own iCloud.

How it works

From idea to itinerary in three steps

Tell us your trip

Pick a destination — your location, an address, or a pin on the map — choose how many days, and what interests you.

We find real places

TripSloth scours MapKit, Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, and OpenStreetMap, then quality-checks every candidate against editorial evidence.

Follow your route

Your day-by-day plan lands with a route that flows, driving access for every stop, and turn-by-turn navigation when you're on the move.
